Research

My research focuses on improving population health through evidence-based approaches to health systems, health services, and stroke care. I am particularly interested in research that connects scientific evidence with local contexts and supports the development of effective, equitable and sustainable health interventions.

Research interests

  • Stroke and cerebrovascular disease
  • Public health and population health
  • Epidemiology and data science
  • Health systems and health services research
  • Health inequalities and equity
  • Evidence-based health policy and practice
